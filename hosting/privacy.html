Privacy Policy — Helicopter War

Package Name: com.hellwar.game  |  Effective Date: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication: When you sign in with Google, we store your Firebase User ID (UID) and pilot display callsign to link and synchronize your game campaign progress across devices.
  • Cloud Firestore Game Data: We store your in-game credits, high score, destroyed enemy barracks records, and Empire Headquarters victories.
  • Google AdMob Advertising SDK: The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK to display Banner, Interstitial, and optional Rewarded Video ads. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and diagnostic ad-performance signals according to Google's Privacy Policy.

2. How Data Is Used

Authentication and Cloud Firestore records are used strictly to authenticate your pilot account, prevent duplicate reward tampering, and restore your campaign progress across devices. Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob to deliver and measure ads.

3. Data Security & Deletion

All network communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption, and Cloud Firestore access is restricted strictly to your authenticated user ID. To request account or campaign data deletion, contact benpasiglandingin@gmail.com.
