Frequently Asked Questions

Troubleshooting tips for Android APK sideloading, installation conflicts, Google Sign-In, and Cloud Firestore progress syncing.

Why does Android block the APK with an "Unknown apps" warning?
Because helicopter-war.apk is distributed directly via GitHub Releases rather than Google Play, Android asks you to approve your browser or Files app once. Tap Settings on the prompt → enable Allow from this source → go back and tap Install.
Installation fails with "App not installed" or a package conflict error
First check that your device runs Android 7.0+ (API 24) and has at least 100 MB of free storage. If you previously installed an older test or debug build of com.hellwar.game, Android will reject updates signed with a different certificate — uninstall the old build first, then install the fresh helicopter-war.apk. Your Google Cloud saves remain intact.
What if my browser renames the download or fails partway through?
Some mobile download managers stall or rename GitHub release redirects. Try downloading directly using Chrome or Android's built-in browser from the GitHub Releases page and make sure the saved file ends in .apk.
How do I fix Google Sign-In errors on the Connection Gate screen?
Helicopter War uses Android Credential Manager with Google Sign-In. Ensure your phone has an active Google account logged in, Google Play Services is updated, automatic Date & time is enabled in Android Settings, and any VPN/firewall is not blocking Google or Firebase endpoints.
How does my campaign progress sync across devices or after reinstalling?
Your War Credits, high scores, destroyed barracks (out of 200), and defeated Headquarters bosses are stored in Cloud Firestore strictly per authenticated user ID (users/{uid}). Sign in with the exact same Google account on your new or reinstalled device to load your synced progress.
Why does the game require an active internet connection to play?
To protect campaign integrity and prevent desynced rewards, barracks destructions, boss victories, and Hangar purchases sync directly with Cloud Firestore in real time. If your connection drops, reconnect to Wi-Fi or mobile data and tap Retry on the Connection Gate screen.
