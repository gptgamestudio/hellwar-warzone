1. Scope & Applicability

This Privacy & Data Handling Policy explains how GPT Game Studio collects, uses, stores, protects, and deletes information when you install or play the Helicopter War: Empire Endless Battle Android application (package com.hellwar.game) or visit its companion web pages.

2. How We Handle Google Sign-In & Firebase Authentication

Helicopter War requires authentication via Google Sign-In (powered by Jetpack Credential Manager and Google Identity Services) backed by Firebase Authentication so that campaign saves remain bound to your account and protected against tampering or unauthorized access.

  • Data Received During Sign-In: When you sign in with your Google Account, Google issues a cryptographic OpenID Connect ID token to Firebase Authentication. Through Firebase Authentication, the app accesses your unique Firebase User ID (UID), your Google display name (used to generate an in-game pilot callsign such as VIPER-7), and your email address / verification metadata within your Firebase Auth session.
  • No Password Storage & Minimal Scope: GPT Game Studio never sees, receives, or stores your Google Account password, contacts, files, Gmail messages, or sensitive Google Workspace data.
  • How Identity Data Is Used:
    • Your uid serves as the sole key scoping your private Cloud Firestore records (users/{uid}).
    • Your email address is displayed locally on your own in-app Tactical Settings screen to indicate which Google Account is signed in and is never written to public collections or leaderboards.

3. How We Handle Cloud Firestore Data

Campaign state is stored in Google Cloud Firestore within a dedicated enterprise database instance, protected by server-enforced Cloud Firestore Security Rules.

Firestore Path / Scope Data Fields Stored Purpose Access & Visibility
users/{uid} userId, callsign, credits, highScore, per-empire barracks counts (humanDestroyedCount, orcDestroyedCount, dragonDestroyedCount, alienDestroyedCount), createdAt, updatedAt Persist your War Credits, career high score, and campaign unlock progression across sessions and devices. Private (Owner-Only): Readable and writable only when request.auth.uid == uid. Listing all users or reading another player's profile is blocked at the database layer.
users/{uid}/destroyed_barracks/{barracksId} userId, barracksId, empire, barracksIndex, damageDealt, creditsAwarded, scoreAwarded, destroyedAt Append-only record preventing duplicate bounty payouts and syncing cleared barracks (up to 200 across 4 empires). Private (Owner-Only): Owner read & idempotent create only; updates and deletes are disabled.
users/{uid}/hq_victories/{empire} userId, empire, bossId, creditsAwarded, scoreAwarded, victoryAt Append-only record confirming defeat of each Empire Headquarters after clearing all 50 barracks in that empire. Private (Owner-Only): Owner read & create upon verified prerequisite; updates and deletes are disabled.
users/{uid}/hangar/state creditsSpentCloud, selectedHelicopterClass, selectedSkin, unlockedHelicopters, upgradesByClass, adContinueWindowStartMs, adContinueUsedToday Store server-validated aircraft ownership, upgrades, skins, cumulative cloud spend, and 24-hour rewarded continue quota across devices. Private (Owner Read / Server Write Only): Readable only by request.auth.uid == uid; mutated exclusively by trusted Firebase Cloud Functions transactions.
rewards_ledger/{nonceId} & sortie_sessions/{sessionId} nonceId / sessionId, userId, rewardType, status, transactionId, timestamps Prevent duplicate AdMob reward claims and verify Server-Side Verification (SSV) callbacks and sortie sessions. Private (Owner Read / Server Write Only): Owner may check own session status; anti-replay tables (ssv_processed_tx) and pre-migration backups (migration_backups) are strictly backend-only.
migration_quarantine/{uid} status, conflict reason codes, preserved unlocked helicopter IDs Non-destructively preserve legacy Hangar aircraft and upgrades whenever local/cloud balances require reconciliation review. Private (Owner Read / Server Write Only): Owner may view notice status; clients cannot edit or delete review records.
leaderboard/{uid} callsign, highScore, totalBarracksDestroyed, hqClearedCount, updatedAt Power the Top 5 Global Pilots ranking on the official companion website and in-game records without exposing private account data. Public Read / Verified Sync: Contains zero PII (no email addresses, real names unless set as callsign, or private credit balances).

Zero-Trust Database Security Rules

  • Default Deny: All unauthenticated database operations and any unmatched collection paths are denied by default (allow read, write: if false).
  • Strict Schema & Bounds Validation: Security rules enforce data types, field allowlists, monotonic progression bounds, and server timestamps (FieldValue.serverTimestamp()) on Cloud Firestore writes.
  • No Player Admin Access: Players cannot view, alter, or bypass Firestore Security Rules, backend configurations, or other players' accounts.

4. On-Device Local Storage & Network Diagnostics

  • Local Preferences (SharedPreferences): Tactical audio/haptics/camera toggles, active mid-stage sortie checkpoints, Hangar aircraft unlocks/upgrades/skin selections, and daily rewarded-ad continue counters are stored locally on your Android device (scoped by your Firebase UID).
  • Network Connectivity Checks: To support real-time cloud synchronization, the app inspects Android network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) and sends lightweight HTTPS 204 reachability checks before entering online gameplay.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

Helicopter War uses trusted Google services to operate authentication, cloud storage, and advertising:

  • Google Play Services, Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore: Governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security Documentation.
  • Google AdMob SDK & UMP Consent: Displays Banner, Interstitial, and optional Rewarded Video ads (including optional Supply Drop and Sortie Continue rewards) only after Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent requirements permit requests. Rewarded ad payouts are verified via AdMob Server-Side Verification (SSV) using ECDSA signatures before backend reward settlement.
  • Managing Ad Consent & Opting Out: Eligible EEA/UK/Swiss users can review or update choices inside the app under Tactical Settings → Privacy & Ad Consent Options. On Android, you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising via Android Settings → Google → Ads.

7. Data Retention Policies & Account Deletion

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide synced gameplay, enforce campaign fairness, and meet legal obligations:

  • Firebase Authentication Records: Your authentication record (Firebase UID, linked Google identity metadata, and sign-in timestamps) is retained while your pilot account remains active, or until you revoke access / request deletion.
  • Cloud Firestore Profile & Subcollections (users/{uid}, destroyed_barracks, hq_victories): Retained continuously while your account is active so your War Credits, scores, and campaign unlocks remain available across reinstalls and devices.
  • Public Leaderboard Projection (leaderboard/{uid}): Non-PII summary statistics (callsign, high score, total barracks destroyed, and HQ clears) remain visible while your account is active and are permanently removed if you request account deletion.
  • On-Device Local Storage (SharedPreferences): Tactical settings, local checkpoints, Hangar state, and 24-hour ad-continue counters reside only on your device and are erased immediately if you clear app data or uninstall Helicopter War.

  1. Open Helicopter War → Tactical Settings and tap COPY USER ID (or note the Google email address used to sign in).
  2. Email benpasiglandingin@gmail.com with your Pilot User ID and/or Google account email.
  3. Upon verifying account ownership, we will permanently erase your users/{uid} document, destroyed_barracks, hq_victories, hangar, and migration_backups subcollections, any linked rewards_ledger/sortie_sessions/migration_quarantine records, your leaderboard/{uid} entry, and Firebase Authentication account within 30 days (unless limited retention is required by law).

You may also revoke Google Sign-In permissions anytime at Google Account Permissions.
