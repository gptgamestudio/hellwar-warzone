Privacy Policy — Helicopter War

Package Name: com.hellwar.game  |  Developer: GPT Game Studio  |  Effective Date: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication: When you sign in with Google, we store your Firebase User ID (UID) and pilot callsign to link and synchronize your game campaign progress across devices.
  • Cloud Firestore Game Data: We store your War Credits, high score, destroyed enemy barracks records, and Empire Headquarters victories.
  • Google AdMob SDK: The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK to display Banner, Interstitial, and optional Rewarded Video ads. Google AdMob may process the Android Advertising ID and ad diagnostics under Google's Privacy Policy.

2. How Data Is Used

Authentication and Cloud Firestore records are used strictly to verify pilot identity, prevent duplicate reward tampering, and synchronize progression across devices.

3. Data Security & Deletion

All network communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption and zero-trust Cloud Firestore rules isolate records per authenticated UID. To request data deletion, email benpasiglandingin@gmail.com.
